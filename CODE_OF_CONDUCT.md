# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing someone's private information.
- Keep issues and pull requests on topic and constructive. Maintainers may edit, hide or close contributions that are not.

Report conduct problems privately through the repository's
[security advisory form](https://github.com/ImYourBoyRoy/periwire/security/advisories/new) (mark it as a conduct report);
the maintainer will respond and can remove content or block users when needed.
